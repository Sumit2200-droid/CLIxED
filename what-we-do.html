04 — Our Way of Working

From evidence to action.

Good educational change is not simply designed. It is tested, implemented, understood and sustained. Every engagement moves through the same six stages.

  1. 01 Research Understand the system as it is.
  2. 02 Design Build with evidence and context.
  3. 03 Implement Support change on the ground.
  4. 04 Evaluate Measure what actually works.
  5. 05 Learn Turn findings into understanding.
  6. 06 Scale Grow what holds.
