04 — Why CLIxED

Six reasons change holds.

We are not a generalist consultancy. We are domain specialists with research strength, policy reach and field credibility — built in large-scale Indian government-school contexts.

Why CLIxED, explained in full →

01

Domain Expertise

Education research, curriculum design, evaluation and professional development — across the full arc of the field.

02

Research Strength

Every claim we make about our work is documented. What we do not yet know, we say so.

03

Policy Engagement

We advise governments and institutions — and translate evidence into briefs and decisions that move.

04

Field Credibility

Findings and designs are produced and tested with teachers and classrooms — not only in papers.

05

Practical Understanding

We understand infrastructure constraints, teacher capacity and the institutional realities that shape what is possible.

06

Understanding of Scale

Large-scale experience across multiple states — with teachers, teacher educators, departments and institutions.
