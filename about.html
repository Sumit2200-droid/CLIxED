01 — Who We Are

Researchers. Designers. Consultants. Practitioners.

CLIxED Foundation is a Section 8, non-profit organisation registered in Bengaluru, India, working at the intersection of research rigour and classroom realities. We are a team of education researchers, curriculum designers, evaluators and consultants who understand education systems from policy papers to blackboards.

Our name echoes our heritage: we grew out of the Connected Learning Initiative (CLIx) and its Education and Development (ED) work — carrying forward a commitment to evidence-led practice in education.

We work on the problems that sit between good intentions and real change in education: how curricula and learning materials are designed, how teachers are supported and developed, how programmes are evaluated for what actually changes, and how research and evidence reach the people making decisions. We partner with governments, institutions, foundations and practitioners across India and beyond — always on the problems of equitable, at-scale learning.

What makes us distinctive is the connection we hold between the two sides of our identity: research that stays honest about what it does and does not know, and practice that is tested in real classrooms — in Indian government-school contexts where change must survive distance, resources and reality.

02 — Our Background

Built on the Connected Learning Initiative.

CLIxED's story runs through a single, direct line of ancestry: CLIxED → CLIx → Tata Trusts — TISS Mumbai — MIT. We are the continuation of that partnership's work — its people, its methods and its hard-won learning.

The Connected Learning Initiative (CLIx) was a major educational initiative: a large-scale, multi-state programme for Indian government schools, aimed at using technology to strengthen learning and teacher development in underserved classrooms. Its scale was ambitious — thousands of teachers and students across multiple Indian states — and its design put teacher agency and classroom practice at the centre of technology-enabled learning.

The initiative was developed and delivered in partnership with the Tata Trusts, TISS Mumbai (Tata Institute of Social Sciences) and MIT (the Massachusetts Institute of Technology) — three institutions whose combined research strength, field reach and technical expertise made the programme possible.

That work earned the UNESCO—King Hamad Prize for the Use of ICTs in Education (2018) — one of the strongest recognitions in the field, awarded for the effective use of information and communication technologies in education. That prize names what CLIx did; the foundation it rests on — research-led design, relentless implementation, honest evaluation, and learning that scales — is the DNA of CLIxED today.

03 — Our Strengths

Why change can be trusted to hold.

Seven strengths, earned in the field — each one a claim we can document, and each one tested against the reality of classrooms that must actually change.

01

Domain Expertise

Deep, lived expertise across the full arc of education — from how teachers learn to how institutions change. Our work is grounded in six connected areas:

  • Teacher education
  • Educational quality
  • Learning design
  • Culture
  • Pedagogy
  • Institutional practice
02

Research Strength

Our research is tested in real educational environments — schools, classrooms, teachers and learners — rather than being purely theoretical. Every claim we make about our work is documented; what we do not yet know, we say so.

03

Policy Engagement

We connect research to policy — advising governments and institutions and translating evidence into briefs, recommendations and decisions that move. Sound policy begins with sound research; our work is built to span that bridge.

04

Communication & Public Engagement

We convert research into public value — commentaries, expert articles, podcasts and public resources that carry evidence from research into practice, for practitioners, policymakers and the wider public.

05

Field Credibility

We have worked inside government-school systems — our findings and designs are produced and tested with teachers and classrooms, not only in papers.

06

Practical Understanding

We understand how policy and programmes actually work in classrooms — including the infrastructure constraints, teacher capacity and institutional realities that shape what is possible.

07

Understanding of Scale

Experience designing, piloting and scaling programmes across states and schools — with teachers, teacher educators, departments and institutions.

04 — What Drives Us

Educational development cannot rely on quick fixes.

We believe lasting improvement in education is built — not bought. It comes from a single, disciplined combination:

Rigorous Research + Institutional Understanding + Honest Evaluation → Sustainable Educational Improvement
01

Learning that actually happens

We measure our work by changes in classrooms — not by documents produced.

02

Evidence over assertion

We make no claim unless it can be documented. What we do not yet know, we say so.

03

Equity as the point
