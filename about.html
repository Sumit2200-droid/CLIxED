About — CLIxED Foundation

Between research rigour and classroom realities.

At CLIxED Foundation (a Section 8 non-profit organisation registered in Bengaluru, India), we work at the intersection of research rigour and classroom realities. We are researchers, curriculum designers, and consultants who build solutions around real constraints - infrastructure, teacher capacity, and institutional challenges.

01 — Who We Are

Researchers. Designers. Consultants. Practitioners.

Our team brings direct, large-scale experience implementing and evaluating major educational initiatives in Indian government school contexts. We were core architects of the Connected Learning Initiative (CLIx), a flagship programme seeded by Tata Trusts, led by TISS Mumbai, with support from Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). CLIx demonstrated how to scale educational technology responsibly across multiple states, reaching thousands of students. That effort earned the UNESCO-King Hamad Prize for the Use of ICTs in Education (2018).

Today, we bring that same evidence-led rigour to policy research, curriculum development, professional learning, and institutional evaluation. Aiming for systemic change that is built to last.

02 — Our Background

Built on the Connected Learning Initiative.

CLIxED's story runs through a single, direct line of ancestry: CLIxED → CLIx → Tata Trusts — TISS Mumbai — MIT. We are the continuation of that partnership's work — its people, its methods and its hard-won learning.

The Connected Learning Initiative (CLIx) was a major educational initiative: a large-scale, multi-state programme for Indian government schools, aimed at using technology to strengthen learning and teacher development in underserved classrooms. Its scale was ambitious — thousands of teachers and students across multiple Indian states — and its design put teacher agency and classroom practice at the centre of technology-enabled learning.

The initiative was developed and delivered in partnership with the Tata Trusts, TISS Mumbai (Tata Institute of Social Sciences) and MIT (the Massachusetts Institute of Technology) — three institutions whose combined research strength, field reach and technical expertise made the programme possible.

That work earned the UNESCO—King Hamad Prize for the Use of ICTs in Education (2018) — one of the strongest recognitions in the field, awarded for the effective use of information and communication technologies in education. That prize names what CLIx did; the foundation it rests on — research-led design, relentless implementation, honest evaluation, and learning that scales — is the DNA of CLIxED today.

03 — Our Scope of Work

What we do.

We serve schools, governments, think tanks, NGOs and private organisations across multiple domains.

01

Educational Consulting

Strategy, programme design, implementation support, and institutional change across the education sector (including Educational Technology initiatives).

02

Design and Development

We design and develop educational programmes, resources, educational technology initiatives, curricula, learning experiences, and content grounded in pedagogical principles and tailored to specific institutional contexts and learner needs.

03

Implementation

On-ground programme delivery, piloting, rollout, and change management to ensure programmes translate from design into effective classroom and institutional practice, with real-time evaluation and capacity building for sustainability.

04

Programme Evaluation

Rigorous evaluation of educational interventions rooted in sound theoretical frameworks and research design, tailored to context and outcome.

05

Professional Development

Workshops and short-term programmes on pedagogy (including subject pedagogies), learning design, design thinking in education, project management, technology integration, educational leadership and other areas related to educational studies.

06

Research and Policy

Original research projects on educational quality, equity, policy, economics of education and institutional practice.

04 — Our Strengths

Why change can be trusted to hold.

Seven strengths, earned in the field — each one a claim we can document, and each one tested against the reality of classrooms that must actually change.

01

Domain Expertise

Our team includes faculty and researchers with deep expertise in teacher education, educational quality, learning design, and the intersection of culture, pedagogy, and institutional practice.

02

Research Strength

Our research is rooted in deep contextual understanding of Indian classrooms and school systems. We test ideas in real educational settings. We pilot, implement, and evaluate programmes ourselves, which means our research informs what actually works in practice. We ask the questions practitioners need answered, not just what academia finds publishable.

03

Policy Engagement

We understand how policy environments shape educational practice and translate our research into actionable policy recommendations. We engage with government agencies, think tanks, and policy bodies to ensure evidence informs decision-making. Our work bridges the research-to-policy gap that often leaves good research unutilised.

04

Communication & Public Engagement

We believe research extends beyond academic circles. We translate complex findings into accessible commentaries, expert articles, podcasts, and public-facing resources that reach practitioners, policymakers, and the broader public.

05

Field Credibility

Each of our founding members has worked inside government school systems, built relationships with teachers and administrators, piloted interventions at scale, and understands the real barriers to adoption and impact.

06

Practical Understanding

We have experienced first-hand how policies translate (or don't) into classroom practice, how programmes interact with existing infrastructure and capacity, and what it takes to achieve sustainable change.

07

Understanding of Scale

Our team members have been involved in designing, piloting, and scaling programmes across multiple states and schools. We understand the infrastructure, capacity, and systemic constraints that determine whether an intervention scales or fails. This experience helps us design for scale from the outset, not retrofit for it later.

05 — Our Strategic Focus

Foresight & Futures Thinking

Beyond our core work in programme design, evaluation, and research, we are building distinctive capability in Foresight and Futures Thinking. We apply foresight methodologies to help educational systems anticipate change and build resilience. As education systems navigate rapid technological, demographic, and policy shifts, we are positioning ourselves to guide stakeholders through multiple possible futures.

07 — What Drives Us

We believe genuine educational development requires moving beyond quick fixes.

It demands rigorous research, deep institutional understanding, and honest assessment of what works and what doesn't in Indian school contexts. We are committed to generating knowledge and capabilities that help educators, policymakers, and institutions create meaningful, sustainable improvements in teaching and learning.

Rigorous Research + Institutional Understanding + Honest Evaluation → Sustainable Educational Improvement
01

Learning that actually happens

We measure our work by changes in classrooms — not by documents produced.

02

Evidence over assertion

We make no claim unless it can be documented. What we do not yet know, we say so.

03

Equity as the point

Scaling learning is only meaningful if it scales equitably — to the schools and learners farthest from advantage.
