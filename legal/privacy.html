01

Who we are

CLIxED ("we", "us") is a Section 8 non-profit organisation registered in Bengaluru, India, engaged in education research, consulting, design, evaluation, policy and professional development. We act as a data controller for the personal data you share via this website, in person, or over the phone.

02

Information we collect

  • Identity: name, date of birth, nationality
  • Contact: email, phone number, address
  • Academic: schools attended, qualifications and professional credentials
  • Professional: work experience, CVs, referees
  • Financial (only when you engage our services): budget ranges and funding arrangements
  • Technical: basic information your browser sends automatically, such as browser type and device type

03

How we use information

  • To understand your context and plan the work you engage us for
  • To respond to enquiries and deliver the research, consulting, design, evaluation and professional-development services you engage us for (with your consent)
  • To communicate with you about your engagement
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We never share, sell or rent your data to third parties for marketing.

04

Legal basis

We rely on consent (mostly), contract (for engagements), and legitimate interest (for client service). You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing info@clixed.org.

05

How long we keep information

Engagement documents: 7 years after project conclusion (Indian tax and compliance norms). Enquiries and assessment forms: 24 months unless you become a client. You can request deletion at any time by emailing info@clixed.org with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

06

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data. We respond within 30 days. Email info@clixed.org with the subject "Privacy Request".

Questions about this policy?

Email us at info@clixed.org.
