01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are commonly used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or gather usage statistics. Some cookies are set by the website itself; others may be set by third-party services embedded in the page.

02

How this website uses them

This website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not use cookies to store any personal data. Browsing this website does not require cookies, and every feature works with cookies disabled in your browser.

03

Third-party cookies

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set cookies in connection with that service, governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not control those cookies.

04

How to control cookies

You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies specifically. Because this website works without cookies, no feature is lost by doing so.
