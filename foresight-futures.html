Why Foresight Matters

Education systems are navigating rapid change.

Technology, demographics and policy are shifting faster than ever. Foresight helps stakeholders think beyond immediate challenges and consider how education systems may need to respond to different possible futures.

Anticipating Change

Three areas of change.

01

Technological Change

Rapid developments in technology can reshape how education is designed and delivered.

02

Demographic Change

Demographic shifts can influence learners, institutions and education systems.

03

Policy Change

Changes in policy environments can affect educational practice and systems.

Exploring Multiple Possible Futures

No single assumed future. Multiple possible futures.

Education systems should not plan around only one assumed future. Foresight & Futures Thinking helps stakeholders consider different possible futures and prepare for uncertainty — strategically, at a high level, before committing to a single path.

Building Resilience

Prepared systems are resilient systems.

Anticipate change → Explore possible futures → Make better-informed decisions → Build more resilient education systems

Our Emerging Capability

A developing capability, built on research.

CLIxED is positioning itself to guide education stakeholders through multiple possible futures — by applying foresight methodologies to educational systems. This is an emerging strategic capability: we are building it deliberately, grounded in the same research strength and evidence-led practice as the rest of our work.
