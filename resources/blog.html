The CLIxED knowledge platform — research reports, case studies, articles and field notes from the field, the policy table and the classroom. Every publication states its methods, sources and limits.

The Platform

A public library of what we learn and build.

CLIxED operates across research, policy, design, evaluation and professional development. This platform is where that work becomes public — findings, methods, cases and conversations, released as they are ready.

Nothing is published here before it is verified. Where material is still in preparation, we say so plainly.
