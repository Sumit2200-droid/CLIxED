What We Do

Seven areas. One standard of work.

Consulting that starts with the institution as it is, and stays with the work until change holds. We work across seven connected areas:

  1. Institutional assessment and strategy development
  2. Curriculum and pedagogical intervention design
  3. Educational technology programme design, implementation support and integration
  4. Professional development and institutional change strategies
  5. Programme/product design consulting
  6. Feasibility studies
  7. Implementation planning

Who We Serve
