What We Do

Consulting work we undertake.

  1. Institutional assessment and strategy development for government school systems
  2. How to design and pilot curriculum and pedagogical interventions
  3. Roadmap for educational technology programme design, implementation support, and integration with existing systems
  4. Professional development and institutional change strategies
  5. Programme/product design consulting for social enterprises, educational publishing houses or EdTech organisations
  6. Feasibility studies and implementation planning for educational initiatives

Who We Serve

Who we serve.

Government and non-governmental organisations, school networks, EdTech companies, international development organisations, and consulting firms seeking educational expertise.
