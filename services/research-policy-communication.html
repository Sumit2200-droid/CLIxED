What We Research

Eight domains. One question: what changes learning?

Every research project builds knowledge and capacity in educational development — grounded in evidence and shaped for the people who will use it.

01

Quality of Teaching & Learning

Research into the quality of teaching and learning in government schools.

02

Teacher Education

Research related to teacher education and professional development.

03

Equity & Access

Research examining equity and access in education.

04

Economics of Education

Research related to resource allocation, cost-effectiveness, educational investment and educational financing.

05

Educational Technology

Research into the design, adoption and use of educational technology in classroom contexts.

06

Policy Analysis

Research examining education policy and its implications.

07

Institutional Practice

Research into how educational institutions operate and implement educational initiatives.

08

Design & Evaluation Methodology

Research into approaches and methodologies for educational design and evaluation.
