Professional Development.

CLIxED builds the capabilities of educators and professionals working in education — by designing and facilitating workshops and short-term programmes that build capacity across multiple areas of educational practice.

Areas of Professional Development

Ten areas of capacity building.

Workshops and short-term programmes across ten connected areas of educational practice:

01

Pedagogy

Develop understanding and application of effective pedagogical approaches.

02

Subject Pedagogies

Professional learning focused on pedagogy within specific subject areas.

03

Learning Design

Build capability to design effective learning experiences.

04

Design Thinking in Education

Apply design principles to teaching-learning challenges and systemic educational issues.

05

Technology Integration

Explore pedagogically sound, practical and context-aware approaches to technology integration in classrooms.

06

Project Management

Develop project management capabilities relevant to educational programmes and initiatives.

07

Research Methodologies

Build knowledge and skills in conducting educational research.

08

Assessment & Learning Analytics

Develop capabilities around assessment and the use of learning analytics.

09

Institutional Leadership

Strengthen educational leadership capabilities.

10

Change Management

Build the capacity required to support institutional change.
