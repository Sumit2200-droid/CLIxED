What We Do / 03

Programme Evaluation.

CLIxED conducts rigorous evaluations of educational interventions — including large-scale EdTech programmes, curriculum reforms and professional learning initiatives. We care not only whether an intervention works, but what works, why it works, and how it can be improved.

Our Evaluation Approach

Methods that stand up to scrutiny.

Our evaluations are grounded in sound theoretical frameworks, based on appropriate research designs, designed to demonstrate clear impact, and intended to generate meaningful insights for improving educational programmes.

Evaluation Types

Five ways we evaluate.

01

Impact Evaluation

Evaluate the impact of educational programmes and interventions and determine whether they produce measurable outcomes.

02

Formative Evaluation

Generate evidence during programme development to identify areas for improvement and strengthen the intervention.

03

Process & Implementation Evaluation

Understand how a programme was implemented, including the barriers and enablers that influence successful implementation.

04

Cost-Effectiveness Analysis

Assess the relationship between the resources invested in an educational intervention and the outcomes achieved.

05

Learning Outcomes Assessment

Assess learner outcomes using validated instruments to understand the learning impact of an intervention.

What You Get

Evidence-backed insights on what works, why it works, and how to improve.

The evaluation provides evidence that helps your organisation understand programme effectiveness, implementation challenges and opportunities for improvement — grounded in data, not assertion.
